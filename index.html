Every day, EV drivers spend idle minutes at drive-thrus, mall parking, and pickup counters — waiting for food, running an errand, or just parked while they shop.

Today, none of that time does anything for the battery. Plug-in chargers ask for a deliberate stop; a five-minute drive-thru queue isn't long enough to justify one. ChargeFlow is built for exactly that gap — the charging you get without changing your day at all.

How it works

Four steps, zero effort for the driver

A transmitter coil sits beneath the pavement, wired to a compact control cabinet. A matching receiver coil mounts to the underside of the vehicle. That's the whole system.

01

Park

Pull into the drive-thru lane or parking stall exactly like you always do — no new habit to learn.

02

Detect

Position sensors confirm the vehicle is aligned over the embedded charging zone.

03

Charge

The coil energizes and power crosses the air gap wirelessly. No cable, no app, no tap to start.

04

Go

Drive away with a topped-up battery — the charge happened while you were already there.

Applications

Anywhere a car already sits still

Drive-thru lanes

A marked stall works like any other — charging starts the moment the driver parks and shops.

Restaurants

Hotels

Overnight parking becomes overnight charging, without running a cable across a walkway.

Fleet & delivery depots

Frequent short stops between routes add up to real range for taxi and delivery fleets.

Technology

Inductive wireless power transfer, engineered for pavement

The same physics behind Qi phone charging, scaled and packaged for embedded outdoor use — a ground-mounted transmitter coil and a vehicle-mounted receiver coil, no physical contact required.
